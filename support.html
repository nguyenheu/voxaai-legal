How can we help?

Find help with VOXA AI: Voice & Reader, your Pro plan, and on-device voice and AI features.

Restore a Pro subscription

  1. Use the Apple Account that originally purchased VOXA Pro for App Store purchases.
  2. Open VOXA Pro in the app and choose Restore Purchases. Keep an internet connection available while Apple verifies your purchase.
  3. If Pro is still unavailable, check that the subscription is active in Settings > your Apple Account > Subscriptions, then reopen VOXA and try again.

Weekly and yearly plans unlock the same Pro features. Pro removes the free daily usage limits for conversations, text reading, and summaries. AI device and model requirements still apply.

Manage or cancel your plan

On iPhone or iPad, open Settings > your Apple Account > Subscriptions > VOXA. You can review your renewal date, change available plans, or cancel renewal there. Deleting VOXA does not cancel a subscription. Prices and any eligible offers appear before purchase.

Apple manages App Store billing. For purchase history, billing help, or a refund request, visit Apple purchase support.

AI is unavailable

VOXA requires iOS or iPadOS 26.0 or later. AI conversations and summaries also require an Apple Intelligence-capable device, Apple Intelligence enabled, downloaded Apple Foundation Models, and a supported language and region. Check the availability message in VOXA and the Apple Intelligence settings on your device. Allow required models to finish downloading before trying again.

A Pro subscription does not enable AI on unsupported devices. Text Reader uses system voices and works independently of AI model availability.

Voice input is not working

  • Allow microphone access for VOXA in iOS Settings. Check speech recognition permissions if your device requests them.
  • Use a supported speech recognition language and allow required Apple speech assets to finish downloading.
  • If recording is interrupted by a call or another app, return to VOXA and try again.

Reading voices and audio export

In Text Reader, enter text, choose an available system voice and reading language, and start playback. Some voices require a download in the device accessibility speech settings. If a voice is unavailable, try another installed voice. Use the app audio export and share controls to save or share the generated audio file.

Your saved content

Chat history, drafts, saved results, and exported audio are stored locally. Deleting a conversation and deleting its saved results are separate actions. Removing the app removes its local data; copies in device backups or files you shared are managed separately. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

Hỗ trợ tiếng Việt

Bạn có thể gửi email bằng tiếng Việt. Để khôi phục gói Pro, mở VOXA Pro và chọn Restore Purchases bằng đúng Tài khoản Apple đã mua. Để quản lý hoặc hủy gia hạn, vào Cài đặt > Tài khoản Apple > Đăng ký > VOXA. Nếu AI chưa sẵn sàng, hãy kiểm tra thiết bị hỗ trợ Apple Intelligence, ngôn ngữ, khu vực và trạng thái tải mô hình. Gói Pro không mở khóa AI trên thiết bị không được hỗ trợ.
