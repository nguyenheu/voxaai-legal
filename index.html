Get started free. Go further with Pro.

Free daily usage is available for conversations, text reading, and summaries. VOXA Pro removes those usage limits. Weekly and yearly auto-renewable plans unlock the same Pro features. Current prices and any eligible offers are shown in the app before purchase.

Manage or cancel renewal in your Apple Account subscription settings. Use Restore Purchases in VOXA to restore an existing Pro subscription. Get help with your plan.

Built around on-device processing

No VOXA account is required. AI responses use Apple Foundation Models on device when available. Your chat history, drafts, saved results, and exported audio are stored locally. Read our Privacy Policy.

Check your device before using AI

VOXA requires iOS or iPadOS 26.0 or later. AI conversations and summaries require an Apple Intelligence-capable device, Apple Intelligence enabled, downloaded models, and a supported language and region. Availability is checked in the app. Pro does not enable AI on unsupported devices.

Text Reader uses system voices independently of AI model availability. Voice input requires microphone access and supported Apple speech assets. Some system assets and voices may need to download. See setup and troubleshooting help.
