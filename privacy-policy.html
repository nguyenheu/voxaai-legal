VOXA AI Privacy Policy

Last updated November 7, 2026

What VOXA processes

VOXA does not require an account or send your prompts, transcripts, conversations, or saved audio to a VOXA server. Voice input is transcribed with Apple Speech, and AI responses are generated with Apple Foundation Models on your device when available. VOXA does not save raw microphone recordings.

What stays on your device

Chat history, saved results, recoverable drafts, app preferences, and audio files are stored in VOXA’s local app data. The current version does not include Firebase, advertising, or analytics SDKs.

Sharing and permissions

VOXA shares a file only when you choose a sharing destination. The receiving app or person then handles it under their own privacy practices. Microphone and speech permissions are used for voice input and can be changed in iOS Settings.

Retention and deletion

Delete conversations in Chat History and saved results separately. Saved results remain when you delete a conversation. VOXA keeps no server-side copy. Removing the app removes its local data; data in device backups remains subject to your Apple account and backup settings.
